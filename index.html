AI-assisted briefings that turn UN meeting transcripts into short, interactive readouts — the consensus, the fault lines, and who said what, each stance traceable to the record. Open a topic below to see its readouts.

How to read these. Each readout is built from the UN transcripts.un.org automatic-speech-recognition (ASR) transcript — a strong draft, not an official UN record. Position-critical quotes are checked against the meeting audio before publication; every readout carries its own source note and a linked methodology page.
